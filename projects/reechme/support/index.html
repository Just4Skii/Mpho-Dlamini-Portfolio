Police and missing persons

There is no waiting period to report a missing person.

South African Police Service guidance is explicit: report a missing person at the nearest police station immediately. A SAPS 55(A) form is completed and signed as part of the report.

SAPS missing-person guidanceCall 10111

What to expect at a police station

  • Ask to report a missing person. You do not need to wait 24 hours first.
  • Bring a recent photograph and any details that could help: clothing, last known location, contacts, and circumstances.
  • The SAPS 55(A) form records the report. Ask for the case number and the officer’s contact details.
  • If a station refuses to take the report, ask to speak to the station commander or contact the provincial missing-person unit.

This summary is not legal advice and is not a substitute for SAPS guidance. The official SAPS page is the authoritative source.

Using this site

Quick exit is a small measure, not a guarantee.

The quick-exit button replaces this page with a weather website. It does not clear your browser history, remove autofill suggestions, delete downloads, or stop device monitoring or parental controls.

If it is safe to do so, use a device you control, a private browsing window, or a trusted person’s phone. Do not enter real names, addresses, health information, or account details into the demo or the contact form.

This public prototype loads no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking scripts. See the privacy page for the full description.

Demo limitations
