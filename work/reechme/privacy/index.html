Privacy and data

A small footprint, stated plainly.

This prototype is built to demonstrate a safety interaction without collecting personal data. This page describes exactly what happens in the browser and what would need to change before any real deployment.

1. The short version

ReechMe is a public product prototype. It is not a live safety service and does not process data on behalf of an organisation. The demo keeps its simulated state in the memory of your current browser tab and clears it when the tab is refreshed or closed.

The contact form is a visual demonstration. It does not make a network request, store what you type, or deliver a message. Support links lead to external organisations with their own privacy practices.

2. The interactive demo

The demo stores fictional notes, contacts, session state, delivery states, and a schematic location area in React memory. It does not write to localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB, or a server. Refreshing the page resets everything.

The names, phone numbers, note contents, event IDs, and locations in the demo are fictional. Any similarity to a real person is unintended. The demo PIN is displayed in the interface and is not a credential.

The site does not request geolocation, camera, microphone, contacts, Bluetooth, USB, or payment access. Security headers deny those browser features. Any map or location sample is an illustration and is explicitly labelled as such.

3. The contact form

The contact form is a non-sending demonstration. When you choose “Preview submission”, the page intercepts the submit event, clears the fields, and displays a confirmation that nothing was sent. There is no fetch call, API route, email service, database, or logging of field values.

Because the form has no recipient, do not use it to disclose anything sensitive or to ask for help. The interface states this next to the form. The same applies to any free-text field in the demo.

4. Hosting and server logs

This repository does not include an analytics service or a database. When the site is served by a hosting provider, that provider may process standard technical request data such as an IP address, user agent, timestamp, and requested URL in order to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

The exact retention and processing of those logs depend on the hosting arrangement, which is not defined by this prototype. A production deployment would need a named hosting provider, a lawful basis, a retention schedule, and a published contact route.

5. Cookies, analytics and third parties

The prototype sets no cookies and loads no analytics, advertising, session-replay, heatmap, or social-media tracking scripts. It does not embed third-party video or maps.

Fonts are bundled by the build process rather than fetched from a third-party font CDN at runtime. The only external destinations are the official support and source links that you choose to open.

6. Support links

Selecting a support phone number opens your device’s dialler. The call is placed by your phone, not by this website. Opening a source link takes you to an external website whose privacy notice applies. ReechMe does not receive information about those interactions.

Support details were checked against official sources on 22 October 2026. See the safety support page for the source list.

7. Children and young people

The prototype is a general-audience product demonstration. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. Because no account or submission exists, there is no age-verification or parental-consent process.

A real service used by minors would require a dedicated safeguarding and privacy design. The demo does not provide that, and the support page lists Childline for children and families.

8. Your choices and rights

Because the prototype does not collect or retain personal data, there is no stored record to access, correct, export, or delete. Refreshing the page clears the demo state. You can avoid the demo entirely and use the public information pages.

If a future deployment introduced accounts or real alerts, it would need a lawful basis, a privacy notice, data-subject request routes, retention rules, and (where applicable) a South African Information Regulator process. None of those are represented here.

9. Changes to this notice

This notice describes the static prototype as built on 22 October 2026. If the site later adds a backend, analytics, a real contact route, or user accounts, this notice must be rewritten before those features are exposed to the public.

Not a production privacy notice

This document is an honest description of a prototype, not legal advice and not a substitute for a POPIA or GDPR compliance review. A real deployment would need a named responsible party and a reviewed privacy programme.
