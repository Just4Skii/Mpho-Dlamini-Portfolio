The philosophy

Most “boosters” kill processes and hope. This one asks Windows to slow idle apps down — and leaves the app you're actually using alone.

EcoQoS is the same mechanism Task Manager exposes as Efficiency mode. Smart Skii-mer applies it to background apps, trims their working sets only when they're idle, and reverses everything when you quit.

Core

Efficiency mode, not termination

Idle background apps get EcoQoS and idle priority through the Windows API. The app in front of you, games, anti-cheat and system processes are never touched. Everything is reverted on exit.

Game-aware focus

Detects fullscreen games, gives the executable a high GPU preference and AboveNormal priority, protects anti-cheat and overlays.

Any AI, or none

OpenRouter, DeepSeek, OpenAI, Groq, Gemini, NVIDIA, or a local model. Keys stay encrypted on the PC. No key? A built-in checker still advises.

Phone remote

Same Wi-Fi. Pair with a 4-digit code, then trigger a clean or switch modes mid-game without alt-tabbing.

Your hotkey

Record any combination for deep clean. Defaults to Ctrl + Alt + D and never conflicts with your game binds unless you choose.

Zero telemetry

No account, no analytics, no background uploads. The only outbound call happens when you press Analyse — and you can see exactly what it sends.

FAQ

Fair questions.

Does it kill my apps? +

No. It asks Windows to lower the scheduling priority of idle background apps and trims their working sets. Foreground apps, games, anti-cheat and system processes are excluded, and changes are reverted when the app exits.

Will it get me banned in a game? +

It only uses documented Windows APIs (EcoQoS, priority classes, per-app GPU preference). It does not inject, hook or modify game files. Anti-cheat processes are explicitly protected from throttling.

Is AI required? +

No. Cleaning, modes and device detection are entirely local. AI is optional and only runs when you press Analyse, or when memory is critical during gaming if you enable that.

Why does Windows warn me? +

The installer is not code-signed yet. SmartScreen flags unsigned executables. Verify the SHA-256 above or build it yourself from source.

Does the phone remote work away from home? +

No — by design. It binds to private network addresses only, so it works on the same Wi-Fi and nowhere else.
